Purpose

When you are connected with your purpose, your tafkid, you can be on the path to balance and fulfillment. Sometimes we get so busy that we lose touch: with ourselves, our families, our children, our spouses, even with our feelings of closeness to the Ribono shel olam.

We sometimes become restless, perhaps even depressed chas v'sholom. We lose motivation. We wonder why we seem to have lost our energy and excitement about life, work, family, Yiddishkeit.

We know something is missing, but what? Sometimes we know exactly what's missing, but can't seem to get out of the stuck alone. And often we simply feel overwhelmed, overburdened, stressed, like we've lost our bechira.

It's quite possible that coaching can help you find the answers to these and many more questions. Coaches work with intelligent people who are committed to the personal growth and development that is sometimes necessary to be the best you that you can be.

Your life is a gift from the ריבון כל העולמים. Have you even started yet?

Always ask yourself: where have I been, where am I going, and how am I going to get there? Who can be on my team? Our goal should be to reconcile the past with the future, בעזרת השם.

You want to partner with a chavrusah for the most important sugya of your life, someone who can help you make the most of it, to recapture the essence that is at the heart of your life, your self, your future.

Discover a purpose. Make your life more fulfilling, enjoyable, and balanced.
